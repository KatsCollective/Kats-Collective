The Story

Hi, I'm Kat

I'm a community care connector — I show up for the vulnerable, the voiceless, and the healing. Whether that's a rescue cat who just needs a chance, a patient facing their final chapter, or a person who simply needs to be heard.

With 15+ years of administrative experience and a lifelong commitment to animals and people, I created this space to gather resources, share stories, and build community around what matters most.

My daily motto? One day at a time. 🌿

What We Stand For

Three Pillars of Purpose

👥

People

Every person deserves to feel seen, heard, and supported — no matter where they are in their journey. From peer support to student advising, we show up.

🐾

Paws

Animals can't advocate for themselves. We help connect communities with rescue resources, TNR programs, and the knowledge to care for our furry family.

🌱

Purpose

Life is richer when we give back. Whether volunteering, sharing your story, or simply being present — purpose is something we can all find together.
